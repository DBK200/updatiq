FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Updatiq?

Updatiq is a status page and maintenance-communication platform. It gives each of your applications a branded public page, lets you communicate incidents and scheduled maintenance, and notifies subscribers by email — all from one place.

Does it have an API?

Yes. Every public page exposes a Statuspage-compatible JSON API (status.json, summary.json, incidents.json, and more), so your existing monitors and integrations work out of the box. There's also a live SVG badge and a one-line embed widget.

Can customers subscribe to updates?

Absolutely. Visitors subscribe by email in one click and receive announcements, reminders before planned maintenance, and completion notices. Every message includes a one-click unsubscribe.

How are incidents different from maintenance?

Maintenance is planned work you schedule ahead of time. Incidents are unplanned issues you communicate as they happen, moving through investigating → identified → monitoring → resolved. Both appear on the status page and drive the overall status indicator.

Do you support teams and permissions?

Yes — organize work into isolated workspaces, invite teammates by email, and assign admin or member roles. A full audit log records every change and security event.
